Schools LMS - layout notes

This version establishes the visual shell only.

Structure:
- Main LMS area is one independent region.
- Right sidebar is a separate fixed div/aside.
- Sidebar is open by default.
- Hamburger is on the LEFT side of the sidebar header.
- School logo placeholder is immediately to the RIGHT of the hamburger.
- Closing the sidebar hides the school logo and navigation labels.
- Navigation icons remain visible when collapsed.
- The actual icon set can be replaced later without changing the layout.
- Student name, roll number, photo, and notification are placeholders for now.
